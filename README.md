## 📌 Executive Summary

The average office worker spends approximately **10 to 12 hours a day sitting down** — amounting to over **3,500 hours a year** and nearly **16 solid years of a 40-year career**. 

This interactive 3D WebGL pitch deck presents the complete ergonomic research pipeline:
`Existing Product` ➔ `User Requirements` ➔ `Anthropometric Data` ➔ `Ergonomic Problems` ➔ `Quantitative Analysis` ➔ `Qualitative Analysis` ➔ `Design Parameters` ➔ `Final Recommendation`

---

## ✨ Features & Interactive Demos

- 🌐 **3D WebGL Spatial Atmosphere**: Built with Three.js featuring dynamic 3D particle fields and spatial camera travel across 8 presentation chapters.
- 🕹️ **Interactive Posture & Disc Strain Gauge** (Slide 4): Live slider simulating spinal disc pressure from `60° Slouch` (180 kg/cm² red alert) to `135° Wakefit Synchro-tilt` (80 kg/cm² optimal emerald glow).
- 🧮 **Interactive Ergonomic Risk Calculator** (Slide 3): Dynamic career sitting time & health risk estimator.
- 🎤 **Presenter Mode & Speaker Notes HUD**: Press **`N`** on any slide to toggle a live translucent speech script drawer.
- ⛶ **One-Click Fullscreen Presentation**: Press **`F`** to enter full-bleed edge-to-edge presentation mode.
- 🔊 **Synthesized Web Audio Soundscapes**: Subtle UI feedback and slide transition audio.
- 📱 **Fully Responsive & 100% Self-Contained**: Works offline or online with embedded base64 high-resolution imagery.

## 🎮 Presenter Controls & Keyboard Shortcuts

| Key | Action | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **`N`** | **Speaker Notes** | Toggles presenter speech script drawer with word-for-word talk track. |
| **`F`** | **Fullscreen Mode** | Enters/exits full-bleed presentation mode (ideal for TV/projector displays). |
| **`→`** or **`Space`** | **Next Slide** | Advances to the next 3D chapter with audio-visual feedback. |
| **`←`** | **Previous Slide** | Moves back to the previous chapter. |
| **`Mouse Move`** | **3D Parallax & Tilt** | Mouse movement tilts 3D glass cards and moves camera perspective. |

## 🏢 How to Present in an Office / Meeting Room

1. **Online (Recommended)**:
   - Connect your laptop to the meeting room display / TV via HDMI or Wireless Display.
   - Open [https://unnati9319-art.github.io/focused-borg/](https://unnati9319-art.github.io/focused-borg/).
   - Press **`F`** for fullscreen and **`N`** for your presenter speech notes.

2. **Offline (No Internet Backup)**:
   - Clone or download the repository.
   - Double click `index.html` to open directly in any browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari). It is **100% self-contained** and requires no server or internet connection.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **3D Graphics Engine**: Three.js (WebGL renderer, particle buffer geometry, spatial perspective camera).
- **Styling & Layout**: Vanilla CSS3 (CSS 3D transforms, glassmorphism, flexbox/grid, custom design system).
- **Audio Engine**: Web Audio API (real-time oscillator synthesis).
- **Deployment**: GitHub Pages.
