Chapter 02 — Physical Damage

What Sitting Does
to Your Body

Sitting puts 40–90% more pressure on spinal discs than standing. Over time: disc degeneration, chronic lumbar pain, and sciatica.
Slouching just 1–2 inches forward exerts up to 30 lbs of extra force on your cervical spine. Shoulders roll forward, chest tightens, hip flexors shorten.
Electrical activity in leg muscles shuts off almost immediately when you sit. Calorie burning drops to just 1 per minute. Blood flow restricts, causing swelling.

"Back pain is not an occupational hazard — it is a hazard of the equipment."
